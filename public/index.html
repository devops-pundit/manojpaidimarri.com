How I think

I borrow the logic of cloud architecture to describe how I work.

Not a wall of service logos. A few useful parallels that explain my engineering habits.

CPUEC2 · Azure VMs

Give the idea compute.

An idea becomes useful only when it can run. I like moving from abstract discussion to something executable, measurable, and testable.

My equivalent: turn thinking into a working system.
↯EventBridge · Event Grid

React when something changes.

Good systems don't need constant supervision. I look for events, signals, and conditions that can move work forward automatically.

My equivalent: connect the dots, remove the waiting.
λLambda · Azure Functions

Automate the small repeatable pieces.

Not every solution needs a giant platform. I prefer small, purposeful pieces that do one job well and compose into something bigger.

My equivalent: simple parts, useful outcomes.
AIAgents · MCP

Give systems a way to reason and act.

My current focus is making cloud tooling accessible to AI agents through clear interfaces, safe actions, and meaningful context.

My equivalent: from automation to intelligent automation.
